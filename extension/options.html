Daf

Highlight on any web page, and the page goes to your Daf Library with your highlights and notes.

Connect to Daf

Make one in Daf: Preferences, Account, Browser extension, Make a token. Daf shows it only once.

When I select text

Only a highlight saves a page to Daf, so selecting text to copy it never does, unless you choose “right away”. Then hold Alt while you select to only select.

Using it

  • Select text on a page and choose Highlight, or press Alt+H. The note button next to it highlights and opens a note.
  • Click a highlight to change its color, add a note and tags, or remove it.
  • Alt+Shift+H, or the list button on a highlight, opens the panel of all your highlights on the page, in the order they come, with their notes and tags.
  • The first highlight saves the page to your Library. The toolbar button saves a page without one and opens it in Daf.
