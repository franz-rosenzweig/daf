# The Vilna page, round 2: notes

- **Best: V4** (`v4.svg`, `best-1024.png`). It is the only version that reads unmistakably as a printed Gemara page at Dock size, with heavy main-text lines and fine commentary lines around them. At 64 px and below it falls back to V1's grid-fitted solid keyhole, so it keeps the best small-size read too.
- **V4 sizes:** one SVG holds three drawings and a media query on the image size picks one. Fine lines are used at 384 px and up (512, 1024), coarse lines from 128 to 383 px (128, 180, 256), and solid blocks at 96 px and below (16, 32, 48, 64). For .icns and Android PNGs, export each size from the SVG as usual and the right drawing comes out.
- **V1** puts every ink edge on the 32 px grid, so the gaps are exactly 1 px at 32 and the keyhole stays sharp there. Paper grain switches off at 64 px and below, on every variation, because at those sizes it only reads as dirt.
- **V2** (corner turned) and **V5** (blue opening word) are V1 plus one idea each. **V6** drops the leaf and is the boldest at 16.
- **Weak, honestly:** V1, V2 and V5 trade round 1's tilt, arch and generous margins for small-size crispness, so at 256 px and up they look blockier than the round-1 pick.
- **Weak, honestly:** V4's lines can read as a generic "text document" icon to someone who doesn't know the layout. Its switch from lines to blocks between 64 and 128 px is a visible change, for example when the Dock magnifies.
- **Weak, honestly:** V3 (open Gemara) is only 46% of the tile tall, so its blocks are the smallest of the six, and at 16 px it is two brown smudges. The uneven main texts keep it from reading as a face, but only just.
- **Weak, honestly:** V6 has no paper, so it loses the "old paper" feeling, and the Rashi block can read as an armchair.
