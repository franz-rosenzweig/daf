# The hour ring: notes

- **Best: V1.** It has 13 parchment pages seen from above, stacked up both sides from a seam at 6 o'clock, where the covers meet. Today's page sits on top at 12. Pages from 12 round to 8 o'clock are in the deeper tone, which is forty minutes of the hour. They are trimmed at a clean round spine.
- **Why V1 wins:** the stacking is mirrored left and right, so nothing turns or swirls. Each page casts one crisp sepia shadow line on the page below it, so the pages can be counted at 256 px and up. At 64 px and below it simplifies to a ring with a two-thirds tone. The object fills about 66% of the tile and is centred at y≈500.
- **V2** has 11 broader pages in one tone and no progress tone. Today's page has its corner turned. The turned corner says "page" best at large sizes, but it vanishes below 64 px.
- **V3** is the view from the spine's end: 18 leaves edge-on. It is honestly the weakest of the three. It reads as a paper rosette or a sunburst, not as a book.
- **Weak points, honestly:** at 64–128 px the ring of radial segments also reads as a citrus slice or a sunflower. At 16–32 px the progress tone makes V1 look like a progress/fitness ring. The pages look like cards as much as paper. Beside Moshe's bold camel it is pale and busier.
