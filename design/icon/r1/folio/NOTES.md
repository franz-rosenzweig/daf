# The folio: notes

- **Best: v2.** The leaf is tilted 4 degrees and its bottom-left corner is curled over the way a Hebrew page turns, so the back (the darker hair side) shows. The tilt and the deeper parchment make it read as a page mid-turn, not a file sitting on a desk.
- **v1** is the calm, upright version in the lighter parchment. **v3** is a wider leaf with a smaller turn: it has more parchment and less gesture.
- **Technique:** two flat tones on the leaf, one gradient (on the turned corner only), a thin lit edge where the turn faces the light, and a contact shadow plus an ambient one. The light comes from above.
- **Fill and centring:** the leaf fills 66 to 68% of the tile's height. Its box is nudged left and down so that its weight, which sits top-right because of the cut corner, lands on the visual centre.
- **At 16 px** it reads as a tan leaf with a dark diagonal corner. The silhouette survives, but the detail does not.
- **Honest weakness:** it is still a blank sheet with a page-curl. To most eyes that sits close to the generic document or "peel" icon. Only the colour and the slightly uneven edges say "parchment", and those edges vanish below 64 px.
- **Also weak:** against Moshe's saturated yellow it looks pale and quiet in the Dock. The cut-away corner leaves the bottom-left of the tile empty.
