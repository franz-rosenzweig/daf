# The sounds and who recorded them

Most of what [Sound](sound.md) plays is Daf's own: a hundred and four recordings people put on [Freesound](https://freesound.org) under Creative Commons 0. That means they gave them away to everyone, no strings attached. Nobody has to credit them for that, but I want to, so here's whose each one is. The other thirty are the ones Chataltul plays, and its [list of sounds](https://github.com/franz-rosenzweig/kavana/blob/master/docs/sounds.md) names who recorded those.

Each one is a stretch of the recording, most of them a minute and a half long (two minutes for the music), with its end blended into its start so it loops without a seam, and it's set to the same loudness as the others of its kind, so a train doesn't come in louder than the rain before it. They're MP3s of about a megabyte and a half, and the browser only downloads one the first time a scene wants it.

`tools/sounds.py` makes them, and its list of picks says which recording each one comes from, where the loop starts and how long it is. So anyone can make them again, or swap one for another recording.
