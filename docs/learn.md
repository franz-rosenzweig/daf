# Learn

Learn is where I go when I want to learn a whole subject rather than read one book. Each subject is an **area**: Dungeons & Dragons, say, or Biblical Hebrew, or writing. An area gathers the books, articles, videos and feeds I've picked for it, keeps them in the order I want to go through them, and has a helper that suggests more. Nothing moves when I put it in an area: a book is still in Books, an article still in the Library, a feed still in the Feed. The area just knows which ones belong to it.

In the new look Learn is one of the tabs, and its pages are **Areas** (with each of my areas under it), **Highlights** and **Review**. In the old look I can pin Learn to the sidebar in Preferences → Sidebar.

## Areas

Areas is a card for each subject, in the area's own colour with a few of its covers on it, how many books, articles, videos and feeds it has, and how long I've spent on it this month. **New area** asks for a name, what I'm after (the goal), a colour, and whether I want suggestions. **Edit** lets me rename an area right on its card, change its colour, move it earlier or later, and delete it. Deleting an area only forgets the gathering; everything that was in it stays in Daf.

## An area's page

The top of an area's page is a panel in its colour with the goal, how many things I've finished out of how many there are, and **Continue**, which opens the next thing I haven't finished. Under it is **Up next**, the next five in order.

Then comes the list of everything in it, with chips for books, articles, videos and the highlights I've made in them. Each one says where I am: New, a percent, or Done. A book the helper suggested that I don't have yet says Wanted, and **Find** opens Add a book with its title. **Edit** lets me move things up and down and take them out, and **Details** changes the name, the goal, the colour and the suggestions.

Beside the list are the area's **Feeds**, with how many posts are new in each, and **Follow** to add a site or a feed.

## Getting things in

There are a few ways:

- **Add**, on the area's page, searches everything in Daf (books, articles, videos) and adds what I pick. Underneath it I can paste a link, which is saved to the Library and goes into the area, or give it a site to follow.
- **Add to Daf** (the + in the corner) has an Area box when I have areas, so a link I save or a site I follow goes straight into one. On an area's page it's already set to that area.
- Anywhere I meet a thing, there's **Add to an area**, with every area to switch on or off. It's in the corner's **⋯** on a book's page, in the **⋯** while I'm reading a book, and in the **⋯** while I'm reading an article or watching a video or listening to an episode, whether it came from the Feed or the Library.
- In the Feed and the Library lists I can pick rows (Select, or hold a row on a touch screen) and **Add to an area** puts them all in at once. With a row picked on a wide screen, its info on the right has the same button.

A post from the Feed gets saved to the Library as it goes into an area, the same as Save to Library would do, since the Feed lets go of what I've seen after a while and the area would lose it. Its highlights and my place go along, and if I'm reading it at the time, the page switches to the saved copy.

## The helper

When an area has suggestions on, the helper looks for books, articles, videos and feeds that someone learning that subject would want, given the goal and what's already there. Each suggestion has **Add** and **×**. Add saves an article or a video to the Library, follows a feed, or puts a book in as Wanted, and in every case it goes into the area. × sets it aside, and the helper remembers not to suggest it again. **Find more** asks again.

The helper only shows a link once it has checked that it opens, and it finds videos on my own Invidious when it can, so I don't get addresses that lead nowhere. It asks the model I picked in Preferences → AI.
