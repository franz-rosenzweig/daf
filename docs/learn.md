# Learn

Learn is where I go when I want to learn a whole subject rather than read one book. Each subject is an **area**: Dungeons & Dragons, say, or Biblical Hebrew, or writing. An area gathers the books, articles, videos and feeds I've picked for it, keeps them in the order I want to go through them, and has a helper that suggests more. Nothing moves when I put it in an area: a book is still in Books, an article still in the Library, a feed still in the Feed. The area just knows which ones belong to it.

In the new look Learn is one of the tabs, and its pages are **Areas** (with each of my areas under it), **Highlights** and **Review**. In the old look I can pin Learn to the sidebar in Preferences → Sidebar.

## Areas

Areas is a card for each subject, in the area's own colour with a few of its covers on it, how many books, articles, videos and feeds it has, and how long I've spent on it this month. **New area** asks for a name, what I'm after (the goal), a colour, and whether I want suggestions. **Edit** lets me rename an area right on its card, change its colour, move it earlier or later, and delete it. Deleting an area only forgets the gathering; everything that was in it stays in Daf.

Next to Areas is **Ask**, the same helper as in an area but across all of them, so I can ask something that pulls from my D&D things and my Hebrew things at once. Its answers say where each part came from too, with **My sources** or **Anywhere**, and the Sources list lets me leave whole areas out. When I ask about something none of my areas is for ("I want to learn pour-over coffee"), it offers a new area with a name and a goal, and **New area** starts it with the helper looking for things to put in it. A book, article or video it offers from outside has **Add**, which asks which area it goes into.

## An area's page

The top of an area's page is a band in its colour with the name, the goal, and how many things I've finished out of how many there are. Under it are the area's four pages: **Overview**, **Everything**, **Plan** and **Ask**.

**Overview** is the area at a glance, in sections: this week of my plan, the thing to continue, what's next in order, my highlights, the questions I've asked, the feeds and the suggestions. Each one only shows when it has something in it. **Rearrange** asks the helper to lay the page out for the subject, and I can tell it what for ("for running my first game", say). It picks the sections, names them, groups my highlights by topic, and can gather a few of my things under a heading of their own, like the videos to watch before a session zero. The new layout shows with **Keep** and **Undo** at the top, and nothing changes until I keep it. **Edit** lets me move sections up and down, take them out and add others, and **Plain** goes back to the page as it was.

**Everything** is the list of everything in the area, with chips for books, articles, videos and the highlights I've made in them. Each one says where I am: New, a percent, or Done. A book the helper suggested that I don't have yet says Wanted, and **Find** opens Add a book with its title. Beside the list are the area's **Feeds**, with how many posts are new in each, and **Follow** to add a site or a feed, and the helper's suggestions. **Edit** lets me move things up and down and take them out, and **Details** changes the name, the goal, the colour and the suggestions.

**Plan** makes me a study plan. I pick how long a day (20 minutes up to an hour) and how many weeks, and the helper builds the weeks out of my own things in an order that builds, splitting a long book into chapters, with minutes for each step. Where the subject needs something I don't have, it adds a step from outside with **Add**, and where it's learned by doing, a step to go and do it. I see the plan first and choose **Keep**, **Again** or **Not now**. Once it's kept I tick steps off as I go, and a step ticks itself when I finish the thing it points to. This week of the plan shows on the Overview.

**Ask** is where I ask the helper about the subject. With **My sources** it answers from what's in the area: my highlights and notes in its books, the articles' text and the videos' transcripts. Under every paragraph it says where that came from, and a tap opens it right there: the highlight, the paragraph in the article, or the moment in the video. With **Anywhere** it can go beyond my things too, and it offers a few books, articles or videos worth adding, each with **Add**. The Sources list lets me leave some of the area's things out of the answer. Each question stays on the page, and the × forgets it.

The helper is the model I picked in Preferences → AI, and an answer, a plan or a layout takes a minute or so. Nothing private goes to it.

## Moving things around, and taking them out

Anything in an area can be moved by its handle, the six dots at its left: I hold it and drag it where I want it, with a finger on the tablet or the phone, or with the mouse. That works for the things in Everything and in Up next, for the Overview's sections, for the plan's steps (from one week to another too), and for the areas themselves on Areas while Edit is on. With a mouse the handles only show when I'm over a row.

The × beside a thing takes it out of the area, right there. It asks once: **Take out**, or **Not a good source**, which takes it out and also tells the helper never to suggest it again. Either way the toast at the bottom has **Undo**, which puts it back where it was. A plan's steps have an × too, with the same Undo.

## Getting things in

There are a few ways:

- **Add**, on the area's page, searches everything in Daf (books, articles, videos) and adds what I pick. Underneath it I can paste a link, which is saved to the Library and goes into the area, or give it a site to follow.
- **Add to Daf** (the + in the corner) has an Area box when I have areas, so a link I save or a site I follow goes straight into one. On an area's page it's already set to that area.
- Anywhere I meet a thing, there's **Add to an area**, with every area to switch on or off. It's in the corner's **⋯** on a book's page, in the **⋯** while I'm reading a book, and in the **⋯** while I'm reading an article or watching a video or listening to an episode, whether it came from the Feed or the Library.
- In the Feed and the Library lists I can pick rows (Select, or hold a row on a touch screen) and **Add to an area** puts them all in at once. On a big screen, the info beside the list has the same button for the row I'm on.

A post from the Feed gets saved to the Library as it goes into an area, the same as Save to Library would do, since the Feed lets go of what I've seen after a while and the area would lose it. Its highlights and my place go along, and if I'm reading it at the time, the page switches to the saved copy.

## The helper

When an area has suggestions on, the helper looks for books, articles, videos and feeds that someone learning that subject would want, given the goal and what's already there. Each suggestion has **Add** and **×**. Add saves an article or a video to the Library, follows a feed, or puts a book in as Wanted, and in every case it goes into the area. × sets it aside, and the helper remembers not to suggest it again. **Find more** asks again.

The helper only shows a link once it has checked that it opens, and it finds videos on my own Invidious when it can, so I don't get addresses that lead nowhere. It asks the model I picked in Preferences → AI.
