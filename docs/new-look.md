# The new look

This is how Daf looks now, on every device. The old look, with its sidebar of icons, is still there for a while: **Preferences → New look → Off** brings it back on the device I'm on, and **On** returns. Adding `?look=1` to the address does the same as Off, and `?look=2` the same as On.

The idea behind it is that each screen has one main thing on it, and everything else waits until I ask for it. Inside a book, an article or a PDF, the bar is the same everywhere: back and Contents on the left, the title in the middle, and the tools on the right as round buttons, the one that's on in the accent colour. With Daf's own theme the page sits as one cream sheet on a gray background, with white cards on it, serif titles and one blue for the buttons that matter. Any other theme in Preferences → Appearance colours it instead: the backdrop a shade darker than the theme, the sheet the theme's page, and Daf's own panels in its accent. The Rooms keep their materials. In the Rustic library the backdrop where the tabs are is the oxblood leather, the sheet is parchment, the cards keep their brass rule, the books stand on their shelves, and a book's bars are stitched leather as they always were; the Wood bookshelf, the Gothic and the Cyberpunk libraries work the same way. Dark mode follows my setting.

## Getting around

There are six tabs: **Today**, **Books**, **Library**, **Feed**, **Learn** and **Listen**. Each one has its own pages in a small row under it: Today and Recovery; Books, All Books, Manga and Torah; Library with its kinds (Articles, Videos and the rest, and my saved views) and the Shortlist; the Edition, the Feed and its folders; Learn's Areas (each area under it), Highlights and the Review; and Listen's own, Listening, Shows, Episodes, Recordings, Audiobooks and Clips, each with its own name at the top. A pinned place only shows when it's pinned in Preferences → Sidebar.

Where the tabs go depends on what I'm holding. On the Mac, or the tablet held upright, they're a row along the top. On the tablet held sideways they're a list down the left, each with its picture, and the tab's pages under it; the button at the foot of the list folds it down to just the pictures, and then the tab's pages move to a row along the top of the page, the way they are when the tablet is upright. On the phone they're a bar along the bottom with five of them, and the rest is in **⋯** at the end of it. It only changes when I turn the device, never by itself.

In the top right corner are **+** to add something, **Search**, the minutes ring (it opens Stats), and **⋯**.

## The corner's ⋯ menu

Each screen keeps the one thing I do there most and what changes what its list shows. Everything else in its bar is in the **⋯** menu: on a list that's Select, Save as a view and Mark seen; on All Books, Columns, Views and the rest; on a book's page, Listen from here, Edit details and the note in Obsidian. Stats, Sources, Preferences and What's new are small at the bottom of every one.

In a book or an article the reader's own **⋯** is tidier too: Listen, Ask and Focus are pictures along the top (their names show when I point at them), under them Notebook, [Margin and Side by side](reader.md#margin-and-side-by-side), then a row of places to go, and the rest in short lines.

## One search

The corner's **Search** looks on the screen I'm on first: it opens that screen's own search in its bar, with **Everywhere** beside it for all of Daf. On All Books and in Preferences it goes to the box that's already there. ⌘K still searches everything at once.

On the Library and Feed lists, **Filter** also holds the order and the tag. On Highlights, one **Filter** button opens the colours, the tag, Favorites, the order and private books.

## A book's own colour

Today opens on one big panel for the book I opened last, in two shades taken from its cover, with a few lines from where I left off, **Continue reading**, and the day's ring. Under it are three cards: what's next (the Edition, the Daily Review, Torah), the rest of the nightstand, and the day so far. This week and the year grids are on Stats instead.

The same colour shows up on Books, on a book's page, on the player while something plays, on the manga I'm partway through, and as a spine on each book's card in Highlights. The Edition's lead story, the parasha and the streak have panels of their own.

The lines from where I left off come from Daf's own place in the book when I've read it here, and otherwise from the Kindle's percent. They only show for EPUBs, and never for a private book.

## Preferences

Preferences is a list of pages down the left, and the one I pick opens beside it, so I'm never scrolling through all of it at once. On the phone it's the list first, and a page opens over it with **‹ Preferences** at the top to go back. The search box still finds a setting anywhere, across every page.

The connected devices and the browser extension have a page of their own under Connections, **Devices & extension**. That's where the downloads for Chrome or Arc and Firefox or Zen are, and where I make the extension's token.
